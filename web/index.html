The problem

Satellites carry emergency communications, disaster imagery, and flood maps, and they matter most in a crisis. Solar radiation storms disrupt them, and most satellites are poorly equipped to respond: small, radiation-sensitive processors, ground contact for a few minutes per pass, and one defense, shutting down into safe mode to wait for instructions.

Lifeline

A few compute-capable hub satellites collect radiation and error telemetry from the fleet, forecast who will be exposed next from the planned orbits, and send every satellite its protective schedule before the radiation arrives. Critical services are handed to shielded peers. Grok acts as the in-flight analyst and briefs the operator by voice.
